roxlap web demo · WebGPU voxel world

Rendered through roxlap-render: the WebGPU compute marcher when your browser has WebGPU, otherwise the CPU opticast path (the chosen backend is logged to the devtools console).
Desktop: WASD / arrows move · Space / Shift up & down · click canvas to mouse-look · B runs a 300-frame bench (results in devtools console).
Mobile: drag the left half of the canvas as a virtual joystick to move; drag the right half to look around.
